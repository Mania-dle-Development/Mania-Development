## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

The highlight of this unit was our collaboration. We were all able to come together, ideate, and develop an interesting project proposal. Communication is key when working in a group project, and for this first unit, our communication was strong (and we plan to keep our communication strong into the future).

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

We had a late start since some of our group members joined the project late. It made it difficult to plan and ideate earlier in the process. However, now that our team has shared contact info, this challenge shouldn’t pop up again in the future.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

In terms of additional support, it would be great to have a lesson on managing user authentication. Unit 8 focuses on passport.js which we are all excited about. More support needs will come as our project blooms and develops.
