## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

Early in development, our team broke down each task to ensure that each of our strengths were best utilized. Some had stronger skills with design and UI/UX. Others had stronger experience with data management. Our strengths led to a strong wireframe, presentation, and data management plan.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

We had to ask difficult questions within our group about the scope and scale of our project. We wanted to make a project we would be proud of, but considering the amount of time we have to develop our project, we had to be realistic about our scope for the project.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?
