## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

During this unit, we were able to successfully get our final and some stretch features finished, tested, and merged into the main branch. We became more communicative and collaborative regarding pull requests, code reviews, and supporting each other in other areas of the project. As time was getting closer to the deadline, our final objectives became clear during the home stretch.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

One of the biggest challenges our group faced in this unit were merge conflicts between certain features and the main branch. We also were all working in crunch time so there was only so much that could be accomplished in such time. Our group also had lost a team member due to inactivity and lack of collaboration, so we had to adapt our numbers as a group to the amount of GitHub issues and work being distributed with just the four of us.

### 3. What were some of the highlights or achievements that you are most proud of in this project?

As a group, we were proud of the ownership of the work that we took in these past few weeks. All four of us all have a feature and story points of our own that we can proudly talk about. With changes occurring throughout development and within group dynamics, we were able to adapt, improvise, and overcome difficult moments individually and as a team. There were also some learning points in software engineering practice that each of us experienced throughout the project, which helped us gain exposure to modern industry and traditional project practices.

### 4. Reflecting on your web development journey so far, how have you grown since the beginning of the course?

Most of us wanted to have frontend experience and came in this course to build a foundation of backend development. Now that this course has concluded, we all have the confidence to create a full stack application with minimal features.

### 5. Looking ahead, what are your goals related to web development, and what steps do you plan to take to achieve them?

Our goals related to web development are to continuously create projects that serve a purpose to help solve a practical or a real-world problem.
